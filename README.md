# Quran Reader

A personal, ad-free Quran reader with recitation audio.

- Full Arabic text (Uthmani script), 114 surahs, browse by Surah or Juz
- Meaning under each ayah in English, Malayalam, Tamil or Hindi (can be switched off)
- Recitation audio per ayah with a choice of reciters (needs internet)
- Bookmarks, resume where you stopped, adjustable text size
- Installable on Android: open the site in Chrome and choose "Add to Home screen"

Text: Noble Qur'an Encyclopedia (quranenc.com) via the quran-json project (CC BY 4.0).
Meanings: English (Saheeh International), Malayalam (Abdul Hameed Madani and Kunhi Mohammed), Tamil (Abdulhameed Baqavi), Hindi (Maulana Azizul Haque Al Umari), as published by QuranEnc.com and the King Fahd Quran Complex; the Malayalam, Tamil and Hindi files come via the fawazahmed0/quran-api collection.
Audio: streamed from the Islamic Network CDN (alquran.cloud) and EveryAyah.
Font: Amiri Quran (SIL Open Font License).
